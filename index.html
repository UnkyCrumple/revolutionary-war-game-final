Revolutionary Soldier

A Survival Story of the American Revolution, 1775–1781

Join the Continental Army

In 1775, farmers, shopkeepers, and young people like you decided to fight for independence from Great Britain. You will take on the role of a soldier and live through 10 real battles.

How the game works
  • Pick 2 skills and 5 supplies for your soldier.
  • In each battle, study the map, then face 3 challenges.
  • Make a smart decision, then roll the dice.
  • You will learn what really happened and why it mattered.
Important

The battles, commanders, and challenges are all real. Your soldier is made up. If your soldier is wounded or killed, you keep playing, but you lose health and points. Earn points to rise in rank from Private to Captain!


For Teachers
  • Time: about 45–75 minutes for all 10 battles. Progress saves automatically in the browser, so students can stop and continue later on the same computer.
  • Reading level: written for grades 4–6. Each battle has a map, key facts, vocabulary, commanders, three decision challenges, a primary source or big idea, a quiz question, and a journal entry.
  • Scoring: survived +100, wounded +50, killed +15, quiz +50. Wounds reduce health; students can never be eliminated. Max score is 3,500.
  • Assessment: students finish with a 5-question reflection (answer 3 or more), then download a .txt file or print / save as PDF to turn in.
  • Discussion idea: compare the choices students made. Why were some choices better? Which supplies helped in which battles?
Maps are simple drawings and are not to scale. Quotes marked “attributed” or “traditionally credited” are repeated in history books but cannot be proven word for word.
